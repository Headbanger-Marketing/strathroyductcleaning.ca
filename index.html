FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

Why is our house so dusty all the time?

In older Strathroy homes it is usually return-side duct leaks pulling dusty basement air into the system. If a good duct cleaning fades within months, ask us to check the duct sealing before you pay for another cleaning.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes. Every job is done by certified technicians under full insurance coverage. Ontario issues no trade licence for duct cleaning, so certification, training, and insurance are what separate real crews from vacuum salespeople, and we carry all three.

Should farm properties around Strathroy clean on a different schedule?

Most do. Homes beside worked fields out toward Melbourne and Glencoe take a beating each spring tillage and fall harvest, and a two year cycle timed after harvest keeps the accumulation from compounding. We will tell you honestly if your sheltered village lot can stretch longer.

Can you remove smoke or pet odours from the ducts?

Yes, and the clearest case is the rental where a smoker lived: tar and stale smoke coat every run, and the smell floods the rooms the moment the furnace cycles. The cleaning does the heavy lifting, agitating and pulling that coating out of the trunk lines and branches. Once the walls are down to metal, a sanitizer finish neutralises the trace that gets baked in. Jump straight to the spray instead and it would lacquer the tar in place, so the sequence is never up for debate.

How often should air ducts be cleaned in Strathroy?

Most homes in our area do well on a two to three year cycle. Farm dust, spring pollen, renovations, and pets all shorten that interval. If the furniture is dusty a day after cleaning, the returns are due.

What is the process for a whole-home cleaning?

The whole duct network gets isolated, put under suction, and cleaned zone by zone. The crew builds the vacuum into the trunk at the furnace, then works outward from there: registers land in the wash basin, the brush goes down each branch so debris walks to the machine instead of drifting back, and the blower compartment gets a hand detail while the system is open. A village bungalow on one furnace is a three to four hour call. Big two-storeys with additions and the century farm places out on the concessions run longer, and we say so at booking, not on the invoice.

We are renovating our century house. Should the ducts be done during or after?

After, or twice if the reno runs long. Drywall sanding puts a fine dust into the air that settles in every run, and it is easier to clear once at the end than to clean mid-project and again at completion. If the walls are already closed up, book the cleaning for the week the contractor leaves.

Do you serve areas outside Strathroy?

Yes. We serve Strathroy-Caradoc and the surrounding Middlesex communities including Mount Brydges, Komoka, Melbourne, Glencoe, and Parkhill.
